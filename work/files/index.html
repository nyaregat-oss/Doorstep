Frequently asked questions

Is Doorstep live yet?

Not yet. This page is a preview. Search results are samples, and sign-ups aren’t saved.

What does it cost customers?

The plan is for searching, comparing, and requesting quotes to be free. Pricing details will be shared before launch.

How do providers get verified?

Providers will confirm their identity and business details. Verified providers get a badge on their profile.

Can I add a service that isn’t listed?

Yes. Categories are configurable, so new ones can be added as providers ask for them.

How do bookings work?

You describe the job and pick a date. The provider accepts, declines, or suggests another time.
