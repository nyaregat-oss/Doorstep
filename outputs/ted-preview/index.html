Designed with care

Clear information builds trust

Doorstep is being built so people can make informed choices before agreeing to a job.

Honest provider details

Profiles and service descriptions come from providers. Verification appears only when an approval is recorded.

Reviews with context

Reviews are intended to follow completed service requests. No demo ratings or testimonials are presented as real.

Clear starting prices

Providers can show a starting price, with a written quote for work that needs more detail.

Requests in one place

Booking details and messages stay attached to the service request so both sides can refer back.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Doorstep live?

This page is a local design preview. Live account and database features require the app dependencies and Supabase configuration.

Are these real providers or prices?

No provider cards are shown as real listings in this preview. Any sample match displayed after searching is clearly marked illustrative.

How do bookings work?

Customers can request a booking or a quotation from a published service. Providers respond from their dashboard; no payment is collected in this preview.
